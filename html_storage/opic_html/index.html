🧠 시험장에서 목숨 걸고 지켜야 할 10대 절대 원칙
규칙 1 Never ask Ava a question (에바에게 직접 질문하지 마라)
시험관인 에바에게 질문을 던지는 것은 부자연스럽습니다. 굳이 묻는다면 수사 의문문(Rhetorical question)만 던지세요.
• Do you like movies, Ava? ❌
• you know what I'm saying? / isn't it? ⭕
규칙 2 Do not use difficult words (어려운 단어 절대 금지! 100% 쉬운 단어만!)
자신 없는 고급 어휘를 쓰려다 버벅거리는 순간 망합니다. 중학생 수준의 가장 익숙하고 쉬운 단어만 사용하세요.
규칙 3 'house' 대신 'home'이나 'apartment' 사용
원어민들은 내가 사는 안락한 주거지를 부를 때 'house'보다 home 또는 apartment를 훨씬 자연스럽게 씁니다.
규칙 4 Try not to repeat the same word in the same sentence
한 문장 안에서 같은 단어를 두 번 연속 쓰지 마세요. 대명사(it, they, them)나 다른 쉬운 말로 대체하세요.
규칙 5 Don't use 2 descriptive/feeling words with 'and'
형용사 2개를 'and'로 이어붙이면 외운 문장처럼 들립니다. 반드시 호흡을 나누어 필러를 넣으세요!
• My room is comfy and cozy. ❌
• My room is just so, you know, comfy. I mean, it's just so cozy. ⭕
규칙 6 Talk about ONE THING and ONE THING ONLY! (딴소리 0%!)
방 좁다는 얘기하다가 "집 근처에 공주대학교가 있다" 같은 관련 없는 내용을 말하면 감점입니다. 질문에 여러 개를 물어봐도 단 하나에만 올인하고 나머지는 1%만 말하거나 버리세요! 답변은 1분 컷!
규칙 7 If you get nervous and have nothing to say, GO RIGHT TO THE CONCLUSION!
말문이 막히고 할 말이 떨어지면 억지로 늘리지 말고 바로 결론 문장으로 넘어가 끝내세요.
"That's why I really love it. I can't explain it!"
규칙 8 Make honest excuses LATER, NOT in the beginning!
"술집 언제 갔냐?" 질문에 "사실 나 술 안 마시는데... 기억 안 나는데..."를 먼저 말하지 마세요. 바로 MP를 때리고, 변명은 맨 나중에 붙이세요.
규칙 9 'alright'가 'OK'보다 훨씬 좋다!
문장 시작이나 전환 시 'OK' 대신 Alright, well...을 쓰면 훨씬 원어민스럽고 자연스럽게 들립니다.
규칙 10 만능 회피 구문: "whether I like it or not, I have no choice but to ~"
부모님과 살거나, 청소/재활용/공부를 해야 할 때:
"Whether I like it or not, I have no choice but to do this." (좋든 싫든 어쩔 수 없이 해야만 해)
✨ IM1~IM2 달성을 보증하는 만능 문법 4종 & 기적의 3문장

1. make me + 동사원형

"It makes me feel confident." / "It makes me feel relaxed."

(그것이 나를 ~한 기분이 들게 만들어줘요)

2. it helps me to + 동사원형

"It helps me to kill time." / "It helps me to recharge."

(시간 때우는 데 / 재충전하는 데 큰 도움이 돼요)

3. This is crucial / important because...

"This is crucial because subway is super reliable."

(Habit 유형에서 행동의 중요성을 어필하는 마법의 연결어)

4. I feel like... & I think

"I feel like my room gives me peace." / "I think it's very safe."

(감정과 의견을 쏟아낼 때 무조건 쓰는 패턴)
💡 기적의 3문장 공식:
• The good thing is that ~ (좋은 점은 ~이다)
• The best thing is that ~ (가장 좋은 점은 ~이다)
• The reason is that ~ (그 이유는 ~때문이다 = because)
💩🔥 [비상 탈출 만능 무기] 똥 & 방구 응급 사건 치트키

과거 경험(Past Experience)이나 롤플레이 13번(문제 해결 경험)에서 어떤 장소(해변, 공원, 자전거, 대중교통, 카페)가 나와도 100% 써먹을 수 있는 원어민 채점관 폭소 보증 치트키입니다!

let it rip / let one rip
방구를 뀌다 (너무 세게 뀌어서 찢어질 듯) → 과거형: I needed to let it rip
cut cheese
방구 뀌다 → I had to cut cheese at Starbucks
thunder from down under
아래에서 천둥이 치다 (방구 마려움) → I felt a little thunder from down under
silent but deadly
소리 없는 방구 (소리 없는데 독함) → It was silent, but deadly.
take a dump / deuce it
똥 싸다 (number 2 = 대변) → I felt like taking a dump / I had to deuce it
turtlehead
똥이 나오기 직전의 초비상 상황! → I was experiencing turtlehead!
unload my beast
화장실에서 볼일 시원하게 보다 → I rushed into Starbucks and unloaded my beast!
as clear as day
너무나 선명하게 (소리가 쩌렁쩌렁) → It was so loud, as clear as day!
💡 왜 이 스토리가 IM1~IM2를 넘어 IH까지 프리패스인가?
학원에서 외운 뻔한 모범 스크립트와 완전히 다르고, 자연스러운 생체 현상과 생생한 감정(embarrassed, relieved, panic)이 듬뿍 들어가 있어 채점관이 "아, 이 응시자는 외운 게 아니라 진짜 자기 언어로 말하는구나!" 하고 최고점을 줍니다!
📚 4대 카테고리별 1분 답변 공식 & 만능 실전 스크립트

📝 Description (묘사 유형) - 1분 구조

공식: What I really love about [주제] is [단 하나의 포인트].
1. 말하고 싶은 것 딱 1개 말하기 (Pure Present 현재형)
2. 어떻게 느꼈는지 (I feel like, I find it quite ~)
3. 왜 느꼈는지
4. 결론: That's why I love it.
🏠 1. 집 묘사 (수납공간 올인)

You know, I gotta say, what I really love about my home is my storage room.

My home isn't all that big, but you see, it's not all that small either. But what I love most is the storage. It's just so, you know, spacious. I mean, it's just so cozy.

None of the size really matters cos I love how big my storage room is. That's why I love my home so much.

👗 2. 한국 패션 묘사 (내 취향으로 전환)

You know, I gotta say, what I really hate about fashion here in Korea is that people tend to only wear safe colors.

I guess you can say, I find it quite boring.

So you see, for me, I try to be as colorful as possible whenever I go out. I guess, you can say that I just like to stand out. That's pretty much my style.

🔄 Habit (습관/루틴 유형) - 1분 구조

공식: 과거 경험을 말하는 것이 아니라 행동 자체만 설명!
1. 행동 설명: No matter what, whenever I ~, I always make sure to ~
2. 중요성 강조: This is crucial / very important because ~
3. 내 감정 및 효과: I love how it helps me to ~
4. Quick Comparison (핵심!): 잠깐 과거로 갔다가 바로 현재로 복귀!
5. I just can't explain it! + That's why로 결론!
🚇 대중교통 루틴

No matter what, whenever I need to go somewhere, I always take the subway. This is crucial because it's super reliable.

I love how I can read something on my phone. It's the only time I actually read! You see, in the past, I never did that, so commuting was boring. But now, it helps me kill time so well. I just can't explain it! That's why I always take the subway.

♻️ 재활용 루틴 (돌발 1위)

Every time I need to recycle, I always check to see that all cans and plastic bottles are crushed to save space.

This is very important. I'm quite lazy, so I take out the recycling only when my bins are full to the max. In the past, I had to recycle nearly every day. But now with my crushing method, I recycle once every 2 weeks! I just can't explain it! That's why I crush everything now.

⏰ Past Experience (과거 경험 유형) - 1분 구조

공식: 서론/배경 0초! 클라이맥스부터 바로 투척!
1. 클라이맥스 선언: I remember this one time when ~
2. Direct Quotation (직접 인용 필수): I was like, "..."
3. 감정 표출 (embarrassed, panicked, relieved)
4. 결론: That's why I could never forget that moment.
🏖️ 해변 방구 썰 (Let it rip!)

Well, alright, let me tell you about a problem of me letting it rip at the beach! It was one of the most embarrassing things I ever had to go through.

We were finding a nice spot, and as I was bending over to pick up my towel, you know, something came out! It was so loud, as clear as day! My friend was laughing so hard, and I was just so embarrassed. But we both laughed so much. That's why I could never forget that moment.

🚲 자전거 똥 썰 (Turtlehead & Starbucks)

Alright, well, the other day, I was riding my bike in the park, and all of a sudden, I felt like I needed to take a dump!

There was no washroom nearby. I was riding very slowly, kind of standing up, if you know what I mean! I was experiencing turtlehead, a total emergency! But luckily, I found a Starbucks. Oh, thank goodness for Starbucks. I rushed in and unloaded my beast. After that, I felt so relieved!

🔀 Comparison (비교 유형) - 1분 구조

공식: 절대 과거부터 말하지 마라! Pure Present MP로 시작!
1. 현재 MP: What I really find interesting about [X] these days is ~
2. 현재 설명 (얼마나 편하고 좋은지)
3. 과거 회상 (1문장만): But you see, in the past, ~ was quite boring / time-consuming.
4. 다시 현재 강조: But now, it's a completely different story.
5. 결론: That's why I really prefer it now.
💡 조명 기술 비교

What I really find interesting about homes these days is that their lighting technology is just phenomenal.

New homes now have lighting systems where you can adjust brightness. But you see, homes before didn't have interesting lighting at all. You couldn't even dim the lights. But now, it's a completely different story. That's why I appreciate modern lighting.

🛒 쇼핑 습관 비교 (쿠팡)

Online shopping has completely transformed how people shop in Korea these days. We have services like Coupang that deliver products within hours.

In the past, people had to physically visit offline stores, which was time-consuming. But now, everything arrives at my door with a few taps. That's why I mostly buy online now.
