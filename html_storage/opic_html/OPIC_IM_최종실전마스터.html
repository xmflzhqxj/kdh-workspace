🧠 시험장에서 목숨 걸고 지켜야 할 핵심 원칙 & 필수 암기 표현
원칙 1 Never ask Ava a question (에바에게 직접 질문하지 마라)
시험관인 에바에게 질문을 던지는 것은 부자연스럽습니다. 수사 의문문만 허용됩니다.
• Do you like movies, Ava? ❌
• you know what I'm saying? / isn't it? ⭕
원칙 2 Do not use difficult words (어려운 단어 절대 금지!)
자신 없는 고급 어휘를 쓰려다 버벅거리는 순간 망합니다. 중학생 수준의 가장 익숙하고 쉬운 단어만 사용하세요.
원칙 3 Try not to repeat the same word in the same sentence
한 문장 안에서 같은 단어를 두 번 연속 쓰지 마세요. 대명사(it, they, them)나 다른 쉬운 말로 대체하세요.
원칙 4 Don't use 2 descriptive/feeling words with 'and'
형용사 2개를 'and'로 이어붙이면 외운 문장처럼 들립니다. 반드시 호흡을 나누어 필러를 넣으세요!
• My room is comfy and cozy. ❌
• My room is just so, you know, comfy. I mean, it's just so cozy. ⭕
원칙 5 Talk about ONE THING and ONE THING ONLY! (딴소리 0%!)
방 좁다는 얘기하다가 "집 근처에 공주대학교가 있다" 같은 관련 없는 내용을 말하면 감점입니다. 질문에 여러 개를 물어봐도 단 하나에만 올인하고 나머지는 1%만 말하거나 버리세요! 답변은 1분 컷!
원칙 6 If you get nervous and have nothing to say, GO RIGHT TO THE CONCLUSION!
말문이 막히고 할 말이 떨어지면 억지로 늘리지 말고 바로 결론 문장으로 넘어가 끝내세요.
"That's why I really love it. I can't explain it!"
원칙 7 Make honest excuses LATER, NOT in the beginning!
"술집 언제 갔냐?" 질문에 "사실 나 술 안 마시는데... 기억 안 나는데..."를 먼저 말하지 마세요. 바로 MP를 때리고, 변명은 맨 나중에 붙이세요.
💬 화제별 필수 암기 필러 및 패턴 표현 모음

🏠 Description (묘사) 전용 암기 표현

• What I really love about ~ is ~ (내가 ~에서 정말 좋아하는 것은 ~이다)

• My home isn't all that big, but you know, it's not all that small either. (우리 집이 그렇게 크진 않지만, 그렇다고 아주 작지도 않아요)

• I find it quite boring / interesting. (나는 그것이 꽤 지루하다고/흥미롭다고 느껴)

🔄 Habit (습관/루틴) 전용 암기 표현

• No matter what, whenever I ~, I always make sure to ~ (무슨 일이 있어도, 내가 ~할 때마다 나는 항상 꼭 ~한다)

• This is crucial / very important because ~ (이것이 매우 중요한 이유는 ~이기 때문이다)

• I just can't explain it! (말로는 설명할 수가 없어!)

⏰ Past Experience (과거 경험) 전용 암기 표현

• I remember this one time when ~ / The other day, I was ~ (언제 한번 이런 적이 기억나요 / 일전에 나는 ~했었지)

• I was like, "..." (내가 이랬지, "...") -> 직접화법 필수!

• That's why I could never forget that moment. (그것이 내가 그 순간을 절대 잊지 못하는 이유야)

🔀 Comparison (비교) 전용 암기 표현

• What I really find interesting about ~ these days is ~ (요즘 ~에 대해 내가 정말 흥미롭게 느끼는 점은 ~이다)

• In the past, ~ but now, it's a completely different story. (과거에는 ~였지만, 지금은 완전히 다른 이야기야)

🌟 만능 필러 및 회피 구문 (모든 화제 공통)

• Alright, well... (OK 대신 시작할 때 쓰는 원어민 표현)

• You know / You see (중간중간 숨 고르기 필러)

• I guess you can say that ~ (내 생각을 자연스럽게 덧붙일 때)

• Whether I like it or not, I have no choice but to ~ (좋든 싫든 어쩔 수 없이 ~해야만 해)

• It makes me feel confident / relaxed. (~한 기분이 들게 만들어)

• It helps me to kill time. (시간 때우는 데 최고야)

📚 화제별 섹션 구분 및 4대 카테고리별 1분 답변 템플릿

📝 Description (묘사 화제) - 섹션별 암기 템플릿

핵심 구조: MP(현재형) ➔ 감정 및 세부 설명 ➔ 결론
🏠 집(Home / Apartment) 화제

You know, I gotta say, what I really love about my home is my storage room.

My home isn't all that big, but you see, it's not all that small either. But what I love most is the storage. It's just so, you know, spacious. I mean, it's just so cozy.

None of the size really matters cos I love how big my storage room is. That's why I love my home so much.

👗 패션(Fashion) 화제

You know, I gotta say, what I really hate about fashion here in Korea is that people tend to only wear safe colors.

I guess you can say, I find it quite boring.

You see, for me, I try to be as colorful as possible whenever I go out. I guess you can say that I just like to stand out. That's pretty much my style.

🔄 Habit (습관/루틴 화제) - 섹션별 암기 템플릿

핵심 구조: 행동 MP ➔ 중요성 ➔ 프리스타일(효과) ➔ 빠른 비교(과거➔현재) ➔ 결론
🚇 교통수단(Transportation) 화제

No matter what, whenever I need to go somewhere, I always take the subway. This is crucial because it's super reliable.

I love how I can read something on my phone. It's the only time I actually read! You see, in the past, I never did that, so commuting was boring. But now, it helps me kill time so well. I just can't explain it! That's why I always take the subway.

♻️ 재활용(Recycling) 화제

Every time I need to recycle, I always check to see that all cans and plastic bottles are crushed to save space.

This is very important. I'm quite lazy, so I take out the recycling only when my bins are full to the max. In the past, I had to recycle nearly every day. But now with my crushing method, I recycle once every 2 weeks! I just can't explain it! That's why I crush everything now.

⏰ Past Experience (과거 경험 화제) - 섹션별 암기 템플릿

핵심 구조: 사건 투척 ➔ 직접화법(Direct Quotation) ➔ 생생한 감정 ➔ 결론
🏖️ 해변 방구 썰 (Let it rip!)

Well, alright, let me tell you about a problem of me letting it rip at the beach! It was one of the most embarrassing things I ever had to go through.

We were finding a nice spot, and as I was bending over to pick up my towel, you know, something came out! It was so loud, as clear as day! My friend was laughing so hard, and I was just so embarrassed. But we both laughed so much. That's why I could never forget that moment.

🚲 자전거 똥 썰 (Turtlehead & Starbucks)

Alright, well, the other day, I was riding my bike in the park, and all of a sudden, I felt like I needed to take a dump!

There was no washroom nearby. I was riding very slowly, kind of standing up, if you know what I mean! I was experiencing turtlehead, a total emergency! But luckily, I found a Starbucks. Oh, thank goodness for Starbucks. I rushed in and unloaded my beast. After that, I felt so relieved!

🔀 Comparison (비교 화제) - 섹션별 암기 템플릿

핵심 구조: Pure Present MP ➔ 현재 설명 ➔ 과거 비교 ➔ 현재 재강조 ➔ 결론
💡 조명 기술 비교

What I really find interesting about homes these days is that their lighting technology is just phenomenal.

New homes now have lighting systems where you can adjust brightness. But you see, homes before didn't have interesting lighting at all. You couldn't even dim the lights. But now, it's a completely different story. That's why I appreciate modern lighting.

🛒 쇼핑 습관 비교

Online shopping has completely transformed how people shop in Korea these days. We have services like Coupang that deliver products within hours.

In the past, people had to physically visit offline stores, which was time-consuming. But now, everything arrives at my door with a few taps. That's why I mostly buy online now.
